# Devin vs Factory

#battlecard

Last updated: 2026-06

---

## Overview

| Dimension | [[Devin]] | Factory |
|---|---|---|
| Company | Cognition ($26B) | Factory AI |
| Type | Autonomous agent + IDE | Enterprise coding agent |
| Focus | General-purpose SWE tasks | Enterprise workflow automation |
| Revenue | ~$492M ARR | Undisclosed (earlier stage) |

## Strengths: Devin

- **Brand recognition** -- first-mover as "AI software engineer"
- **Broader capability** -- handles diverse tasks from tickets to migrations
- **Scale** -- 4,000+ organizations, major enterprise customers
- **Product suite** -- Devin + Windsurf + DeepWiki
- **Funding** -- $2.5B+ raised, well-capitalized

## Strengths: Factory

- **Enterprise focus** -- purpose-built for enterprise software development workflows
- **Custom agents** -- specialized agents for specific tasks (testing, documentation, etc.)
- **Compliance** -- designed with enterprise security and compliance in mind
- **Integration depth** -- deep integration with enterprise toolchains

## Weaknesses: Devin

- Broader focus may mean less depth in specific enterprise workflows
- Higher price point

## Weaknesses: Factory

- Smaller market presence and brand recognition
- Less public information about capabilities and customers
- Earlier stage -- less battle-tested at scale

## Sales positioning

Factory competes in the enterprise agent space but with a narrower focus. When encountering Factory in deals, emphasize Devin's **breadth of capability**, proven enterprise customers (Goldman Sachs, Mercedes-Benz, U.S. military), and the combined Devin + Windsurf + DeepWiki platform.

## Questions to validate

- What specific enterprise workflows is the prospect trying to automate?
- Is the prospect evaluating specialized vs. general-purpose agents?
- What compliance requirements does the prospect have?
